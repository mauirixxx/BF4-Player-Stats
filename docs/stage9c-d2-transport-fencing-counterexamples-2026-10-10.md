Two adversarial counterexamples are now explicit:
1. A valid gateway-generation check at t=0 does not prevent physical transmission after a fence at t=1 if the process pauses between checking and sending.
2. A write already queued in a simulated transport can transmit after the control plane increments its fencing generation. Logical fencing alone cannot retract the write.

The model also demonstrates why a queued attempt cannot be marked safe merely because the gateway was fenced. Any assertion that a transport is drained must rely on external, transport-specific proof. The `transport_drained_proven` flag is an explicit assumption, not evidence generated by the model.

**Next design decision:** define an operator-reviewable enforcement architecture that prevents collector bypass and accounts for outstanding queued transmissions during gateway shutdown/failover. Prefer a fail-closed design with no automatic release of uncertain capacity. Do not claim a hard physical-send guarantee until the network/transport boundary is proven and tested.
