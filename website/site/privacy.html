Privacy Policy

How BF4 Player Stats handles public game data and operational information.

Effective October 28, 2026

Public game information

We collect and display publicly available Battlefield 4 and Battlelog data, including player names, aliases, platform and persona identifiers, gameplay statistics, platoon names, rosters, and emblems. These records may be retained for historical comparisons and may not immediately reflect changes at their sources.

Discord bot information

Bot features may process Discord guild, channel, user, and command identifiers, along with configuration and operational activity needed to provide commands, notifications, and troubleshooting.

Website requests and security

We may process IP addresses, request times, browser information, and server logs for security, diagnostics, rate limiting, and abuse prevention. Short-lived session cookies may be used for request validation and CSRF protection. Public statistics do not require a website account.

How information is used and shared

Information supports searches, rankings, profiles, platoon rosters, Discord responses, caching, and system administration. Public game data is displayed to visitors. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process operational data to deliver the service. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Retention and safeguards

Public records, aliases, and statistics may be stored for historical and operational purposes. Log and cache retention varies by system needs. Access controls and security measures are used, but no internet service is entirely risk-free.

Corrections and removal requests

To request correction, exclusion, or removal of information relating to you, contact us through the Support page and include the relevant profile or platoon link. Requests are reviewed individually, subject to applicable law and technical limitations. Removing a record here does not remove it from third-party sources.

Other services and updates

EA, Battlelog, Discord, and external links have their own privacy practices. This project is not intended to collect private information from children. We may update this policy and its effective date as the project changes. Contact us through the Support page.
